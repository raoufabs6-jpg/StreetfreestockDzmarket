## The sessions

### 🟡 SUNDAY — GYM: UPPER BODY

*Hypertrophy focus · dumbbells & cables*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Target muscles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dumbbell Bench Press | 3 × 8–10 | 90s | CHEST, TRICEPS, FRONT DELTS |
| 2 | Seated Cable Row | 3 × 10–12 | 90s | LATS, RHOMBOIDS, BICEPS |
| 3 | Dumbbell Shoulder Press | 3 × 10–12 | 90s | FRONT DELTS, SIDE DELTS, TRICEPS |

**1. Dumbbell Bench Press** — 3 × 8–10 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Lying flat on the bench, shoulder blades pinned, elbows tucked to 45°.
- **Finish:** Pressing the dumbbells up until they meet over the chest, elbows just short of lock.
- **Coaching cue:** Lying flat, elbows at 45° — press dumbbells up over the chest.
- **Targets:** CHEST (gold) · TRICEPS (gold) · FRONT DELTS (gold)

**2. Seated Cable Row** — 3 × 10–12 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Seated tall, arms extended, slight knee bend, torso upright.
- **Finish:** Handle pulled to the lower stomach, shoulder blades squeezed together.
- **Coaching cue:** Arms extended, slight knee bend — row the handle to the lower stomach.
- **Targets:** LATS (gold) · RHOMBOIDS (gold) · BICEPS (gold)

**3. Dumbbell Shoulder Press** — 3 × 10–12 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Seated with back support, dumbbells racked at shoulder level, wrists stacked.
- **Finish:** Pressed overhead to full lockout, ribs down, no lower-back arch.
- **Coaching cue:** Seated, dumbbells at shoulder level — press overhead until arms lock.
- **Targets:** FRONT DELTS (gold) · SIDE DELTS (gold) · TRICEPS (gold)


### 🔵 MONDAY — CALISTHENICS: FUNDAMENTALS

*Bodyweight patterns · floor work*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Target muscles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Standard / Knee Push-Ups | 3 × 6–10 | 90s | CHEST, FRONT DELTS, TRICEPS |
| 2 | Australian Pull-Ups | 3 × 8–10 | 90s | MID-BACK, LATS, BICEPS |
| 3 | Forearm Plank | 3 × 30–45s | 60s | RECTUS ABS, DEEP CORE, GLUTES |

**1. Standard / Knee Push-Ups** — 3 × 6–10 · 90s rest

- **Start:** High plank, hands under shoulders, glutes and core locked, body in one line.
- **Finish:** Chest lowered to roughly 1 inch from the floor, elbows tucked to ~45°.
- **Coaching cue:** High plank, core tight — lower the chest to 1 inch off the floor.
- **Targets:** CHEST (cyan) · FRONT DELTS (cyan) · TRICEPS (cyan)

**2. Australian Pull-Ups** — 3 × 8–10 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Hanging under a low bar, arms extended, heels on the floor, body straight.
- **Finish:** Chest touched to the bar, shoulder blades retracted, elbows driven back.
- **Coaching cue:** Inverted row: body straight, pull the chest up to the low bar.
- **Targets:** MID-BACK (cyan) · LATS (cyan) · BICEPS (cyan)

**3. Forearm Plank** — 3 × 30–45s · 60s rest

- **Start:** Forearms and toes down, elbows under shoulders, spine neutral.
- **Finish:** Static hold — pelvis tucked, ribs down, glutes tight. Time is the target.
- **Coaching cue:** Static hold on forearms and toes, pelvis slightly tucked.
- **Targets:** RECTUS ABS (cyan) · DEEP CORE (cyan) · GLUTES (cyan)

### 🟡 WEDNESDAY — GYM: LOWER BODY

*Squat hinge press · free weights & machine*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Target muscles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goblet Squat | 3 × 10–12 | 90s | QUADS, GLUTES, CORE |
| 2 | Romanian Deadlift | 3 × 10–12 | 90s | HAMSTRINGS, GLUTES, ERECTORS |
| 3 | Leg Press | 3 × 12–15 | 90s | VASTUS LAT., VASTUS MED., GLUTES |

**1. Goblet Squat** — 3 × 10–12 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Standing tall, dumbbell held vertically against the chest, feet shoulder-width.
- **Finish:** Deep squat with elbows tracking inside the knees, chest up, heels planted.
- **Coaching cue:** Dumbbell held vertically at the chest — sink between the knees.
- **Targets:** QUADS (gold) · GLUTES (gold) · CORE (gold)

**2. Romanian Deadlift** — 3 × 10–12 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Standing with dumbbells at the thighs, shoulders back, knees softly bent.
- **Finish:** Hips pushed back, torso hinged to mid-shin, hamstring stretch — spine neutral.
- **Coaching cue:** Standing with dumbbells — hinge the hips back, soft knees.
- **Targets:** HAMSTRINGS (gold) · GLUTES (gold) · ERECTORS (gold)

**3. Leg Press** — 3 × 12–15 · 90s rest

- **Start:** Seated, knees bent to 90°, feet flat and shoulder-width on the platform.
- **Finish:** Sled driven up until the legs are almost straight — never slam the knees.
- **Coaching cue:** Seated in the machine, knees at 90° — drive the sled up.
- **Targets:** VASTUS LAT. (gold) · VASTUS MED. (gold) · GLUTES (gold)


### 🔵 THURSDAY — CALISTHENICS: PULL + CORE

*Vertical pull · scapular control*

| # | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest | Target muscles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Negative Pull-Ups | 3 × 4–6 | 120s | LATS, BICEPS, GRIP |
| 2 | Scapular Pulls | 3 × 8–10 | 60s | LOWER TRAPS, LATS |
| 3 | Hollow Body Hold | 3 × 20–30s | 60s | UPPER ABS, LOWER ABS, CORE |

**1. Negative Pull-Ups** — 3 × 4–6 · 120s rest

- **Start:** Jump or step up so the chin clears the bar, shoulders engaged.
- **Finish:** Lowering under control over 3–5 seconds to a full dead hang. Reset each rep.
- **Coaching cue:** Jump to chin-over-bar — lower over 3–5 s to a dead hang.
- **Targets:** LATS (cyan) · BICEPS (cyan) · GRIP (cyan)

**2. Scapular Pulls** — 3 × 8–10 · 60s rest

- **Start:** Dead hang from the bar, arms straight, shoulders relaxed by the ears.
- **Finish:** Shoulders pulled down and back, chest lifting slightly — elbows never bend.
- **Coaching cue:** Dead hang — pull shoulders down and back, elbows straight.
- **Targets:** LOWER TRAPS (cyan) · LATS (cyan)

**3. Hollow Body Hold** — 3 × 20–30s · 60s rest

- **Start:** On the back, lower back pressed down, arms and legs elevated off the floor.
- **Finish:** Static hold — ribs down, arms by the ears, legs low but lumbar flat. No arch.
- **Coaching cue:** Lying on the back, press the lower back hard into the floor.
- **Targets:** UPPER ABS (cyan) · LOWER ABS (cyan) · CORE (cyan)

- **Start:** Hands on the floor, knees resting on the upper triceps, gaze slightly forward.
- **Finish:** Leaning forward until the feet lift clear of the floor and balance is held.

- **Start:** Push-up position with the feet against the wall, core braced.
- **Finish:** Feet walked up the wall, hands walked in — supported handstand, ribs down.
- **Coaching cue:** Push-up position, feet on the wall — walk the hands in.
- **Targets:** SHOULDERS (gold) · UPPER CHEST (cyan) · CORE (cyan)

**3. Deep Squat Hold** — 3 × 45–60s · 60s rest

- **Start:** Standing, feet slightly wider than the hips, toes turned out a little.
- **Finish:** Static hold at the absolute bottom of the squat — chest up, heels down.
- **Coaching cue:** The deepest possible squat, chest up, knees tracked out.
- **Targets:** HIPS (gold) · ANKLES (cyan) · GROIN (cyan)

## Rules of execution

1. **Warm up first — always.** 5–8 minutes of easy movement, then 2 lighter sets of the first exercise before your working weight.
2. **Leave 2–3 reps in reserve** in weeks 1–4. Stop the set when the next rep would slow down or break form, not when you hit failure.
3. **Tempo:** 2 seconds down, 1 second up, no bouncing. Control is the training stimulus — the weight is just the tool.
4. **Rest periods are part of the prescription.** Use a timer. Short rest compromises the next set more than a light load does.
5. **Add reps before you add weight.** Hit the top of the rep range on all sets, then increase the load by the smallest available increment.
6. **Log every session.** Weight, reps, and how hard it felt. The log is what makes week 5 different from week 1.
7. **Never train through joint pain.** Muscle soreness is normal; sharp or localised joint pain means stop and regress the movement.

## Scaling guide

| Situation | Regression / substitution |
|---|---|
| Push-ups too hard | Knee push-ups or incline (hands on a bench). Regress until you can own 3 × 8 clean reps, then progress. |
| Australian pull-ups too hard | Raise the bar height so the torso is more upright, or bend the knees and plant the feet closer. |
| Negative pull-ups too hard | Use a box to step to the top and lower over 3 seconds only — or substitute 3 × 5 slow Australian pull-ups. |
| Plank / hollow hold too hard | Reduce the hold time before you reduce the quality. 20 perfect seconds beats 45 sloppy ones. |
| Wall walks too hard | Start with pike holds against a wall, then walk one step up and back down under control. |
| No dumbbells available | Any pressing/rowing implement works — machines, kettlebells, resistance bands. Keep the movement pattern and the rep/rest prescription. |

## Recovery checklist

- **Sleep 8+ hours.** This is the single largest recovery lever at 58 kg — tissue repair and CNS recovery both happen here.
- **Eat in a surplus.** A surplus of roughly +300 to +400 kcal over maintenance is what turns training into new body mass.
- **Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg** of bodyweight daily, spread over 3–5 meals.
- **Walk on rest days.** Easy blood flow speeds recovery without adding training stress.
- **Deload weeks 7 and 12.** Same movements, half the volume. Do not skip them — they are when adaptation is consolidated.
